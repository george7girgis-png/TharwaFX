Not sure which to pick?

New to trading?

Start with Standard. Costs are in the spread, so they are easy to read.

Trading often?

Raw Spread usually costs less at higher volume.

Avoiding swap?

Choose Islamic. A fair-use policy applies.

Questions

Can I change account type later?

Yes. Open another account from your client area and transfer funds between them.

Which currencies can I hold?

Accounts can be held in USD, AED or EUR*. You choose the base currency when you open the account.

Is there a demo for each account?

Yes. A demo is available for Standard, Raw Spread and Islamic, with the same conditions and virtual funds.
