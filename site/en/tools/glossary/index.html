Trading terms, explained

Plain definitions in English and Arabic.

Ask سعر الطلب

The price at which you can buy.

Balance الرصيد

Money in your account, not counting open positions.

Base currency العملة الأساسية

The first currency in a pair. In EURUSD it is the euro.

Bid سعر العرض

The price at which you can sell.

CFD عقد الفروقات

A contract that pays the change in an asset's price, without owning the asset.

Commission العمولة

A set fee per lot, charged on Raw Spread accounts.

Drawdown التراجع

The fall from a peak in your account equity to a low point.

Equity حقوق الملكية

Your balance plus or minus the profit or loss on open positions.

Expert advisor المستشار الخبير

An automated trading program that runs inside MetaTrader 5.

Free margin الهامش الحر

Equity that is not tied up as margin and can open new positions.

Gap الفجوة

A jump in price between one quote and the next, often at the market open.

Hedging التحوط

Holding opposite positions to reduce risk.

Instrument الأداة

Anything you can trade, such as a currency pair, index or stock.

Leverage الرافعة المالية

Borrowed exposure that lets you control a larger position than your deposit.

Lot اللوت

The standard trade size. One lot of forex is 100,000 units of the base currency.

Margin الهامش

Money set aside to keep a leveraged position open.

Margin call نداء الهامش

A warning that your equity is close to the level needed to hold your positions.

Market order أمر السوق

An order to buy or sell straight away at the best available price.

Micro lot لوت صغير

1,000 units of the base currency, or 0.01 of a standard lot.

Order الأمر

An instruction to open or close a position.

Pip النقطة

The fourth decimal place on most currency pairs, or the second on yen pairs.

Pending order الأمر المعلّق

An order that waits until the price reaches a level you set.

Quote currency عملة التسعير

The second currency in a pair. In EURUSD it is the US dollar.

Requote إعادة التسعير

A new price offered when the market moves before your order fills.

Slippage الانزلاق السعري

The difference between the price you asked for and the price you got.

Spread الفارق السعري

The difference between the bid and ask prices.

Stop loss وقف الخسارة

An order that closes a position at a set loss.

Stop-out الإغلاق القسري

Automatic closing of positions when margin level falls too low.

Swap رسوم التبييت

Interest charged or paid for holding a position overnight.

Take profit جني الأرباح

An order that closes a position at a set profit.

Volatility التقلب

How much and how quickly a price moves.

Volume الحجم

The size of a trade, in lots.
