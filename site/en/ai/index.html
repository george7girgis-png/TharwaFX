Questions about AI trading

Will the AI make me money?

No tool can promise that. The AI follows rules and limits, and markets can still move against them. Simulations and backtests show possibilities, not results.

Can I try it without risking money?

Yes. Every AI tool will run on a demo account with virtual funds first, so you can watch it work before going live.

Who is responsible for the trades?

You are. You choose the strategy and the limits, you can pause it any time, and every order shows the reason it was placed.

Is it available in Arabic?

Yes. Analysis, news summaries and trade explanations are written in Arabic or English, your choice.
